## Farmer's Delight official starting guide

URL: https://github.com/vectorwing/FarmersDelight/wiki/Getting-Started

The author's guide, edited in 2021, explicitly assumes current default settings and warns that modpacks may differ. Its useful starting structure is flint knife, gathering secondary materials and discovering wild crops, before more kitchen equipment. It says a knife gathers straw from grassy plants and provides secondary drops from animals. Knives have no wood or stone tier. Advancements provide optional clues. This supports a meaningful low-cost entry rather than suggesting a costly pot is needed to make vanilla mushroom stew. Exact drops and recipes still need selected-version reconciliation.
