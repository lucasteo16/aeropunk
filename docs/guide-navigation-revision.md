# Revised player navigation

## Status

This revision supersedes the homepage grouping in the earlier taxonomy draft. The complete metadata inventory remains valid as a coverage reference, but its category assignments and page memberships must be reconciled with this navigation before implementation. This is an editorial revision, not a pack change.

## Homepage categories

- Automation and industry. Ore processing, manufacturing, mechanical power, electricity, fuel production, automated farms, automated kitchens and automated trading. Focus on machines doing work and transforming resources.
- Storage and logistics. Backpacks, containers, bulk storage, inventory sorting, filters, belts, funnels and distribution networks. Focus on keeping items and moving them to the right place.
- Food and farming. Hunger, saturation, dietary variety, crops, cooking utensils, recipes and regional cuisines. Link automated production to its machinery pages rather than duplicating instructions.
- Building and decoration. Building materials, copycat blocks, furniture, decorative machinery, palettes, randomized placement, schematics, paintings and displays.
- Vehicles and travel. Flying ships, boats and submarines, trains, other transport, teleportation and deferred space travel. Include passenger use as well as construction and operation.
- Combat and character. Weapons, combat controls, spells, classes, skill trees, accessories and equipment improvement. Include preparation and tactics for bosses, with links to finding their encounters.
- Exploration and adventure. Landscapes, dimensions, villages, dungeons, creatures, boss locations, maps and expedition planning. Explain what exists and how to find it.
- Player utilities and quality of life. Carrying blocks, portable sleeping, death recovery, harvesting conveniences, item inspection, control search and other useful interactions. Storage capacity stays in Storage and logistics, not here.
- Visuals and sound. Resource packs, shaders, distant terrain, animations, particles, weather, dynamic lighting, interface appearance and audio. Explain what players can configure and which choices may cost performance on their machine.

## Ownership rules

A topic has one canonical article and may be linked from several activity pages. Machine operation and processing belong to Automation and industry. General item routing and storage belong to Storage and logistics. An ore-processing article links to routing articles for its inputs and outputs, but logistics does not own the processing recipe.

Fuel production belongs to Automation and industry. Vehicle fuel requirements belong to vehicle operation and link back to production. Crop growing and manual cooking belong to Food and farming. Automated production belongs to Automation and industry, with prominent links from food pages. Decorative building tools belong to Building and decoration even when technically convenient player tools.

Boss discovery belongs to Exploration and adventure; fighting and equipment advice belong to Combat and character. These are different player questions rather than duplicate articles. Death recovery belongs to Player utilities and quality of life, linked from combat, exploration and vehicle pages.

Two category levels maximum. The next selection opens an article. Direct activity links and search provide shortcuts without requiring a player to understand every category boundary.

## Secondary references

Optimization internals, administrator tools and commands, libraries and compatibility form a separate reference area, not more equally prominent homepage cards. Player-facing graphics and performance settings remain reachable from Visuals and sound. No category implies mandatory progression.

## Language

English and Simplified Chinese only. Search behavior and English aliases under the Chinese game language remain unverified.
