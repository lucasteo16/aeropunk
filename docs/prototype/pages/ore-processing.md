# Process ores

Turn raw materials into useful outputs with Create machines. Choose a process by its recipe, not by assuming a larger machine always gives more metal.

## Try raw iron

Crushing raw iron produces crushed raw iron, with a chance of an experience nugget. Washing the crushed material produces iron nuggets, with a chance of redstone. Compare the live recipes before choosing what to build.

Search `@create` in the item browser, then look for Crushing Wheel and Encased Fan. This broad query helps you browse the mod; its exact behavior under both game languages is part of the prototype test.

## Choose what you want to inspect

Use the Crushing Wheel's existing Ponder demonstration to learn its arrangement. Inspect the Encased Fan demonstration for bulk processing. The finished page will provide direct buttons; those actions are not implemented in this first content draft.

Input delivery and output collection belong to Storage and logistics. This page will link there instead of repeating the routing instructions.
