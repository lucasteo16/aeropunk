## Stats and pet food

Some foods grant attribute bonuses in addition to hunger. Their tooltips identify the affected attribute and bonus. By default, the addon enables these bonuses and allows further servings to add the bonus again.

| Item | View | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <ItemLink id="lendersdelight:improved_dog_food" /> | <ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="lendersdelight:improved_dog_food" /></ItemGrid> | Hold and use on a tamed wolf |

Improved dog food is a pot dish from cooked koboleton meat and a koboleton bone. Use it on a tamed wolf and the wolf heals to full and gains the food's effects.
