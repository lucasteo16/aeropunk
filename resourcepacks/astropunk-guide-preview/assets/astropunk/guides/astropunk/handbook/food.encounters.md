# Encounter food

## Cataclysm ingredients

<EmiSearch query="@lendersdelight" /> <EmiSearch query="@cataclysm" />

<ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="cataclysm:amethyst_crab_meat" /><ItemIcon id="cataclysm:coral_chunk" /><ItemIcon id="cataclysm:lionfish" /><ItemIcon id="lendersdelight:deepling_meat" /><ItemIcon id="lendersdelight:raw_brute_deepling_meat" /><ItemIcon id="lendersdelight:raw_warlock_deepling_meat" /></ItemGrid>

L_Ender's Cataclysm Delight turns Cataclysm creatures into ingredients. Small and mid tier mobs drop their own meat, and bosses drop parts that only boss dishes use. The mod adds more than fifty foods and seven boss dishes.

Kill a creature with a knife when you want portions instead of a whole cut. <ItemLink id="cataclysm:amethyst_crab_meat" /> becomes slices and sticks. <ItemLink id="cataclysm:coral_chunk" /> and <ItemLink id="cataclysm:lionfish" /> feed the coral and reef branch. Deepling meat comes in three variants, one for each deepling type.

| Ingredient | Source family | First prepared form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="cataclysm:amethyst_crab_meat" /></ItemGrid> | Amethyst crab | Meat slice and meat stick |
| <ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="cataclysm:coral_chunk" /></ItemGrid> | Coral golem | Red slice and coral sandwich |
| <ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="cataclysm:lionfish" /></ItemGrid> | Lionfish | Slice and grilled lionfish |
| <ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="lendersdelight:deepling_meat" /></ItemGrid> | Deepling | Cooked deepling meat |

A dish recipe is not proof of a guaranteed drop. Kill the creature once and read the actual drop before you plan a meal around it.

## Boss dishes

<ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="lendersdelight:netherite_monstrosity_meat" /><ItemIcon id="lendersdelight:cooked_netherite_monstrosity_meat" /><ItemIcon id="lendersdelight:maledictus_heart" /><ItemIcon id="lendersdelight:maledictus_heart_stew" /><ItemIcon id="lendersdelight:leviathan" /><ItemIcon id="lendersdelight:ignis" /><ItemIcon id="lendersdelight:watcher_heart" /></ItemGrid>

Boss meat is a separate tier. A boss drops a raw part, the kitchen turns it into a cooked cut, and a few parts become a named dish. <ItemLink id="lendersdelight:netherite_monstrosity_meat" /> cooks into a cut. <ItemLink id="lendersdelight:maledictus_heart" /> becomes a heart stew or a glazed heart. <ItemLink id="lendersdelight:leviathan" /> and <ItemLink id="lendersdelight:ignis" /> are plated boss dishes that use the whole encounter ingredient.

Eating these dishes feeds the mod's stat progression. Each new dish adds a small permanent gain, so a varied boss menu is worth more than repeating one recipe.

| Stage | Item | What it needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raw part | <ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="lendersdelight:netherite_monstrosity_meat" /></ItemGrid> | A defeated boss |
| Cooked cut | <ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="lendersdelight:cooked_netherite_monstrosity_meat" /></ItemGrid> | Heat and a pan or pot |
| Named dish | <ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="lendersdelight:maledictus_heart_stew" /></ItemGrid> | The part plus a full recipe |

## Cooking route

<EmiSearch query="@farmersdelight" />

<ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="farmersdelight:cooking_pot" /><ItemIcon id="farmersdelight:skillet" /><ItemIcon id="farmersdelight:cutting_board" /><ItemIcon id="farmersdelight:flint_knife" /><ItemIcon id="minecraft:bowl" /></ItemGrid>

Most of this menu runs through the same three stations as the rest of the pack. Cut portions on a <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:cutting_board" />, cook single pieces in a <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:skillet" />, and combine full recipes in a <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:cooking_pot" />. Bring a stack of bowls, because soups and stews need one per serving.

Carry the raw part home rather than cooking at the dungeon. Boss ingredients are heavy in recipe cost, and a failed field cook wastes the trip.
