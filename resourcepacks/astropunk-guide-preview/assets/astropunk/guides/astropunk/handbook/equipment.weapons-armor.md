## Getting started

Choose a weapon family, then compare its material tiers, armor attributes and associated abilities in the item browser. Armor entries above show the chest piece for each named set; inspect the head, legs and feet separately. Boss equipment does not automatically grant class progression. Compatibility equipment for absent expansions is not included here.

[Accessories](equipment.accessories.md), [bosses](adventure.bosses.md) and [skills](combat.skills.md).
