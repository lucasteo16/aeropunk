# Local transport

## Hypertubes

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="create_hypertube:hypertube" />
  <ItemIcon id="create_hypertube:hypertube_entrance" />
  <ItemIcon id="create_hypertube:hypertube_accelerator" />
  <ItemIcon id="create_hypertube:hypertube_junction" />
  <ItemIcon id="create_hypertube:tube_scanner_attachment" />
</ItemGrid>

Connect two tube blocks by right-clicking one while holding another, then place the second. Entrances need at least sixteen revolutions per minute. Accelerators also support braking; splitters connect up to three tubes and use movement keys for direction. Scanner and redstone detector attachments supply detection and mode switching. Use Hypertube Ponder for the connections.

***

## Walkways

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="escalated:metal_walkway_steps" />
  <ItemIcon id="escalated:wooden_walkway_steps" />
</ItemGrid>

Escalated supplies metal and wooden moving walkway steps for local transport. Use their recipes and available help to build the walkway rather than treating them as railway track.
