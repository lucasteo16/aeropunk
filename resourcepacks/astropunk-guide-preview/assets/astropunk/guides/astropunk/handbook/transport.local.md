Connect two tube blocks by right-clicking one while holding another, then place the second. Entrances need at least sixteen revolutions per minute. Accelerators also support braking. Splitters connect up to three tubes and use movement keys for direction. Scanner and redstone detector attachments supply detection and mode switching. Use Hypertube Ponder for the connections.
