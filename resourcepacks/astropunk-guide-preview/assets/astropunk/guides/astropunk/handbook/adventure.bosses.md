# Bosses

## Infernal Dragon

<EmiSearch query="@block_factorys_bosses" />

![Infernal Dragon](images/encounters-bossesrise-infernal-dragon.png)

Ashlord, The Infernal Dragon. Bosses'Rise. An orange winged dragon with a long tail.

Find Dragon Tower in eligible Overworld jungles, plains and savannas.

Enter the tower. Its boss spawner controls the encounter.

***

## Yeti

![Yeti](images/encounters-bossesrise-yeti.png)

Skor, The Yeti. Bosses'Rise. A pale, bulky creature with blue ice around its limbs.

Find Yeti Hideout in the Overworld Snowy Plains.

Reach the arena inside the hideout.

***

## Sandworm

![Sandworm](images/final-boss-review-sandworm.png)

Sirok, The Sandworm. Bosses'Rise. A segmented worm that moves beneath the sand.

Find Sandworm Nest in an Overworld desert.

Enter the nest.

***

## Underworld Knight

![Underworld Knight](images/encounters-bossesrise-underworld-knight.png)

Helvar, the Underworld Knight. Bosses'Rise. A dark armored knight with a large sword.

Find the underground Underworld Arena in the Nether Wastes or Soul Sand Valley.

Reach the arena.

***

## Kraken

![Kraken](images/final-boss-review-kraken.png)

Nerakyss, The Kraken. Bosses'Rise. A tentacled sea monster.

Find Kraken Ship in deep Overworld oceans, including cold and lukewarm waters.

Explore the ship. Its pirate encounter spawner controls the encounter.

***

## Ignis

<EmiSearch query="@cataclysm" />

![Ignis](images/encounters-l_enders-cataclysm-ignis.png)

L_Ender's Cataclysm. A fiery armored warrior carrying a sword and shield.

Find Burning Arena in the Nether Wastes.

<ItemGrid>
<ItemIcon id="cataclysm:burning_ashes" />
<ItemIcon id="cataclysm:altar_of_fire" />
</ItemGrid>

Place one <ItemLink id="cataclysm:burning_ashes" /> in <ItemLink id="cataclysm:altar_of_fire" />. The offering is consumed when Ignis appears.

***

## Netherite Monstrosity

![Netherite Monstrosity](images/nav-visual-cataclysm-netherite-monstrosity.png)

L_Ender's Cataclysm. A horned giant with enormous articulated hands.

Find Soul Black Smith in the Nether Wastes, Soul Sand Valley or either Nether forest.

Damage the dormant giant to wake it. No summoning offering is required.

***

## Ender Guardian

![Ender Guardian](images/encounters-cataclysm-ender-guardian-publisher.png)

L_Ender's Cataclysm. A massive purple and gold guardian.

Find Ruined Citadel on the End Highlands and End Midlands.

<ItemGrid>
<ItemIcon id="cataclysm:altar_of_void" />
</ItemGrid>

Approach within six blocks of <ItemLink id="cataclysm:altar_of_void" /> on a difficulty other than Peaceful. No offering is required.

***

## The Harbinger

![The Harbinger](images/encounters-cataclysm-the-harbinger-publisher.png)

L_Ender's Cataclysm. A dark mechanical boss with red lights.

Find Ancient Factory beneath the Overworld in eligible Terralith caves.

<ItemGrid>
<ItemIcon id="minecraft:nether_star" />
</ItemGrid>

Use one <ItemLink id="minecraft:nether_star" /> on the dormant Harbinger. It is consumed in survival.

***

## The Leviathan

![The Leviathan](images/nav-visual-cataclysm-the-leviathan.png)

L_Ender's Cataclysm. An aquatic creature with four mouth sections and a tongue.

Find Sunken City beneath deep Overworld oceans.

<ItemGrid>
<ItemIcon id="cataclysm:abyssal_sacrifice" />
<ItemIcon id="cataclysm:altar_of_abyss" />
</ItemGrid>

Place one <ItemLink id="cataclysm:abyssal_sacrifice" /> in <ItemLink id="cataclysm:altar_of_abyss" />. The offering is consumed when the Leviathan appears. Prepare for an underwater fight.

***

## Ancient Remnant

![Ancient Remnant](images/nav-visual-cataclysm-ancient-remnant.png)

L_Ender's Cataclysm. A large creature with a long segmented tail.

Find Cursed Pyramid in an Overworld desert.

<ItemGrid>
<ItemIcon id="cataclysm:necklace_of_the_desert" />
</ItemGrid>

Use one <ItemLink id="cataclysm:necklace_of_the_desert" /> on the dormant Remnant. It is consumed in survival.

***

## Maledictus

![Maledictus](images/nav-visual-cataclysm-maledictus.png)

L_Ender's Cataclysm. A horned, winged warrior equipped with maces, a bow and a halberd.

Find Frosted Prison in the Overworld Snowy Plains.

<ItemGrid>
<ItemIcon id="cataclysm:strange_key" />
<ItemIcon id="cataclysm:door_of_seal" />
<ItemIcon id="cataclysm:cursed_tombstone" />
</ItemGrid>

Defeat Aptrgangr for <ItemLink id="cataclysm:strange_key" />. Use it on <ItemLink id="cataclysm:door_of_seal" /> to reach the chamber. Interact with <ItemLink id="cataclysm:cursed_tombstone" /> while holding an item once its power has recovered. The tombstone does not require a specific offering.

***

## Scylla

![Scylla](images/nav-visual-cataclysm-scylla.png)

L_Ender's Cataclysm. A humanoid warrior with sea snakes and a chained anchor.

Find Acropolis over the Overworld Warm Ocean.

Interact with the inactive Scylla to begin the encounter. No specific offering is required.

***

## Invoker

<EmiSearch query="@illagerinvasion" />

![Invoker](images/final-boss-review-invoker.png)

Illager Invasion. A spellcasting illager boss. Find it during village raids. The wave and difficulty depend on server settings.

***

## Wildfire

![Wildfire](images/encounters-friends-and-foes-forge-wildfire.png)

Friends&Foes. A blaze-like creature surrounded by broad golden shields. Find it in a Nether Citadel. Enter the structure to encounter it.

***

## Ender Dragon

![Ender Dragon](images/final-boss-review-ender-dragon.png)

Minecraft. A black winged dragon on the central End island. Reach it through an activated End portal.

***

## Wither

![Wither](images/final-boss-review-wither.png)

<ItemGrid>
<ItemIcon id="minecraft:soul_sand" />
<ItemIcon id="minecraft:soul_soil" />
<ItemIcon id="minecraft:wither_skeleton_skull" />

Minecraft. A floating three-headed skeleton. Build a T-shaped frame from four <ItemLink id="minecraft:soul_sand" /> or <ItemLink id="minecraft:soul_soil" />. Place three <ItemLink id="minecraft:wither_skeleton_skull" /> across the top, placing the final skull last.
