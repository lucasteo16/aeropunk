## Getting started

Use the accessory interface supplied by Curios to inspect slot restrictions. Jewelry adds rings and necklaces; Relics adds items with passive, triggered or activated effects. Compare the complete tooltip rather than equipping by rarity alone. The Jeweler’s Kit is the crafting entry for Jewelry.

</ItemGrid>
