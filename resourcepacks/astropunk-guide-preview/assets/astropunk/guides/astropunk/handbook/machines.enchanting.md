## Integration scope

Brass Bookshelf and Infuser require Apothic Enchanting. Blaze Composer, Affix Augmentor and Gem Cutter require Apotheosis. Neither prerequisite is included in the light or heavy edition, so these integration machines are not part of their enchanting catalog.

***

## Getting started

Make a Mechanical Grindstone first. Its Ponder entry shows the Item Drain conversion and experience input. Follow the Blaze Enchanter entry before spending liquid experience. Super enchanting can cause lightning strikes, so consult that entry before enabling it.
