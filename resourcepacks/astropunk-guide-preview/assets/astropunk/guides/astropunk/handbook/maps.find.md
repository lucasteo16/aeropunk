## Getting started

### Biome finder

<Recipe id="naturescompass:natures_compass" />

Craft <ItemLink id="naturescompass:naturescompass" /> for biome searches.

### Structure finder

<Recipe id="explorerscompass:explorers_compass" />

Craft <ItemLink id="explorerscompass:explorerscompass" /> for structure searches.

Find the compass in Browse recipe, craft it, then use it to choose a destination. Follow its bearing and distance. Save a waypoint once you arrive.

- [Browse recipe](help.search.md)

Search limits and server restrictions can affect results. Use Dimensions to choose the destination dimension first.

- [Dimensions](world.dimensions.md)
