# Space navigation

## Planetary display

<EmiSearch query="@rocketnautics" />

![Hologram planetary display](images/cosmo-hologram.png)

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="rocketnautics:hologram_block" />
</ItemGrid>

Place a <ItemLink id="rocketnautics:hologram_block" /> to view the celestial system. Short-click its configuration points to adjust the scale. It displays the Overworld, Moon, Mars, gas giant and ice world. A visible celestial body is not necessarily a landable destination.

The table is an overview, not a destination selector or automatic pilot.

<Recipe id="rocketnautics:hologram_block" />

## Sputnik Probe

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="rocketnautics:sputnik" />
</ItemGrid>

<ItemLink id="rocketnautics:sputnik" /> keeps chunks loaded and reports biome colors for cartography. These functions are separate from piloting a route.

<Recipe id="rocketnautics:sputnik" />

***

## Helmet display

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="rocketnautics:space_helmet" />
</ItemGrid>

In Deep Space, <ItemLink id="rocketnautics:space_helmet" /> shows an orbital display. Toggle its alignment helper with <KeyBind id="key.rocketnautics.toggle_alignment" />, whose default is Y. This changes the display, not the ship's steering. The helmet has no crafting recipe.
