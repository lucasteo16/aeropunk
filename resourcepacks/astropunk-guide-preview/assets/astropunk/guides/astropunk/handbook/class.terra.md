# Terra Wizard

![YouTube](images/tutorial-video.png) [English](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Minecraft+1.21.1+Elemental+Wizards+RPG+Terra+Wizard+abilities+equipment+tutorial+English) · [中文](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=%E6%88%91%E7%9A%84%E4%B8%96%E7%95%8C+1.21.1+Elemental+Wizards+RPG+%E5%9C%9F%E7%B3%BB%E6%B3%95%E5%B8%88+%E6%8A%80%E8%83%BD+%E8%A3%85%E5%A4%87+%E6%95%99%E7%A8%8B+%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87)

## Role & first weapon

Terra Wizards use earth wands and staves for protection, aimed attacks and ground effects.

![Elemental Wizard robes](images/visual-elemental-robes.png)

### What to look for

Earth spells scale with Earth power. The Clay Wand names Earth power, and the recipe costs a clay ball and a stick, so the first focus is available before any mining trip. Read the school line before you bind a book.

Earth abilities in the book are split between protection and aimed attacks. Stone Flesh and Earth Golem are the defensive options, while Impale and Terra Circle need a target. A build that uses both needs runes for both kinds of cast.

The Terra Wand and the Terra Staff are the specialist upgrades, and the staff adds raw iron work. The Elemental Staff names Air, Earth and Water power at once but needs a nether star, so it is a late goal.

The Dripstone Robe supports the school with pointed dripstone and wool, and its chest piece also needs a dripstone block. The Mountain Robe is a later upgrade that uses an epic armor upgrade and a warden upgrade crystal. The Elemental Robe covers all three elemental schools.

A successful first use looks like this. Hold the Clay Wand, cast at one aimed target and confirm the hit, then cast Stone Flesh and check that its protection shows on you before a fight rather than during one. If the armour value does not change, the spell did not cast and its rune was not spent.

### Readiness

The hints below are optional. Each stage describes what you can already do, not how long you have played.

| Stage | Ready when | Next decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early game | You have clay and a stick, and the rune the wand spell names. | Craft the Clay Wand and cast once. Add Elemental Robe pieces when wool is spare. |
| Early midgame | A Spell Binding Table with bookshelves is reachable and its cost is payable. | Bind the Terra book and equip it with a compatible focus held. |
| Midgame | Pointed dripstone is available and you can compare the Terra Wand with the Terra Staff. | Choose the Dripstone Robe path or stay with the general robe by the school bonus each piece gives. |
| Late game | You can obtain an epic armor upgrade or a nether star. | Treat the Mountain Robe and the Elemental Staff as separate goals, and upgrade one piece at a time. |
