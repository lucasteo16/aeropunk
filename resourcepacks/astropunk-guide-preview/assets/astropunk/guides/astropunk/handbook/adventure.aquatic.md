## Other encounters

<EmiSearch query="@spawn" />

Great White Sharks also have shipwreck encounter definitions. A beached shipwreck is not evidence that a shark can survive on dry land. Not every named entity is ordinary wildlife. Goldfish, Hagfish, Nautilus, Pearlfish, Umbrella Octopus, Vampire Squid, Beakling, Shell Beast, Karcinogen, Karcinoma and Karkinos have no ordinary biome-spawn entry in this catalog. Their separate acquisition or encounter conditions are not confirmed here.

Swimming wildlife, item drops, bucket capture and Starcatcher rod catches are different acquisition routes. Check the species and recipe before treating them as interchangeable. The Starcatcher bridge extends rod catches without changing the natural-spawn habitats listed here. Bring air supplies when exploring deeper water and avoid assuming every aquatic creature is harmless.
