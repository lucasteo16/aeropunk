## First steps

<ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="spell_engine:spell_binding" /></ItemGrid>

<ItemLink id="spell_engine:spell_binding" />

<Recipe id="spell_engine:spell_binding_table" />

1. Open a class article and craft its starter weapon or focus. Weapon-provided abilities are separate from class-book abilities.
2. Create and bind the matching class book at a Spell Binding Table, then equip it in its spell-book slot with compatible held equipment. Check the displayed experience, lapis, bookshelf and casting-resource requirements.
3. Open <KeyBind id="key.puffish_skills.open" /> and read connected nodes and incompatible roots before spending. Class points, weapon points and ordinary experience levels are separate. Changing equipment does not refund points.
