# Engines

## Aviation engines

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="aeroengineering:engine_fan" />
  <ItemIcon id="aeroengineering:engine_compressor" />
  <ItemIcon id="aeroengineering:engine_combustor" />

  <ItemIcon id="aeroengineering:engine_exhaust_cone" />

Aero Engineering supplies turbofan, afterburning turbofan, variable airflow, vectoring, high bypass, turboprop, gas turbine and turboshaft families. The basic core uses Fan, Compressor, Combustor, Turbine and Exhaust Cone in that order. It burns Aviation Kerosene. Open the engine Ponder for casing, nozzle, fuel delivery and gearbox placement.

## Propulsion components

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="createpropulsion:thruster" />
  <ItemIcon id="createpropulsion:vector_thruster" />
  <ItemIcon id="createpropulsion:solid_fuel_thruster" />

  <ItemIcon id="createpropulsion:ion_thruster" />
  <ItemIcon id="createpropulsion:stirling_engine" />
</ItemGrid>

Create Propulsion supplies standard, vector, solid fuel, liquid vector and ion thrusters; Stirling engines, solid and liquid burners; wings, tempered wings and copycat wings. Creative thrusters are a separate creative family. Match each engine to its own recipe and fuel or power help rather than assuming interchangeable inputs.

Aviation Alloy ingots and sheets, Engine Blades, Compressor Components and Turbine Components supply the aviation material family. Create Propulsion supplies platinum ore, raw platinum, ingots, nuggets, sheets, casings, tanks and vessels, plus pine resin, turpentine and oxidizer. Its standard Thruster uses Turpentine and redstone, Solid Fuel Thruster burns solid items from the rear, and Ion Thruster needs Forge Energy and redstone.
