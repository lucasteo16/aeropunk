Aviation materials supply the engine core parts. Create Propulsion has a separate platinum and fuel material family. Its standard Thruster uses Turpentine and redstone, Solid Fuel Thruster burns solid items from the rear, and Ion Thruster needs Forge Energy and redstone. The bucket icon represents the fluid, not a requirement to insert a bucket.
