Gather chorus fruit on the End’s outer islands after gaining access beyond the central island. Cutting one fruit gives four Chorus Fruit Grains. Grain, sauce and dried flowers support different drink and dessert recipes.

- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:chorus_fruit_grain" /> Cut on the cutting board with a knife using 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:chorus_fruit" />. Yield 4.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:chorus_fruit_milk_tea" /> Combine in a heated cooking pot using 1 <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:milk_bottle" /> or another accepted milk plus 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:chorus_fruit" /> or an accepted substitute. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:bubble_tea" /> Combine in a heated cooking pot using 1 <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:milk_bottle" /> or another accepted milk plus 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:chorus_fruit" /> or an accepted substitute plus 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:ender_pearl_grain" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:chorus_fruit_popsicle" /> Arrange in the crafting grid using 4 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:chorus_fruit_grain" /> plus 2 <ItemLink id="minecraft:ice" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:stick" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:chorus_fruit_wine" /> Combine in a heated cooking pot using 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:sugar" /> plus 2 <ItemLink id="minecraft:chorus_fruit" /> or an accepted substitute. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:dragon_breath_and_chorus_soup" /> Combine in a heated cooking pot using 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:suspicious_stew" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:dragon_breath" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:dried_chorus_flower" /> plus 2 <ItemLink id="minecraft:chorus_fruit" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:chorus_flower_tea" /> Combine in a heated cooking pot using 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:ghast_tear" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:dried_chorus_flower" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:dried_chorus_flower" /> Cook in a furnace using 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:chorus_flower" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:chorus_fruit_pie_slice" /> Cut on the cutting board with a knife using 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:chorus_fruit_pie" />. Yield 4.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:chorus_flower_pie" /> Combine in the crafting grid using 1 <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:wheat_dough" /> or another accepted dough plus 2 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:dried_chorus_flower" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:poppy" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:sugar" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:chorus_sauce" /> Combine in a heated cooking pot using 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:chorus_succulent" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:chorus_fruit" /> or an accepted substitute. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:chorus_cookie" /> Combine in the crafting grid using 2 <ItemLink id="minecraft:wheat" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:chorus_fruit_grain" />. Yield 8.

Shulker meat requires knife hunting, making End cities an ingredient destination. Cutting one piece gives two slices. Endermite and enderman ingredients form separate branches and are not substitute shulker meat.

- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:shulker_meat" /> Kill a shulker while holding a Farmer’s Delight knife. End cities provide access to these creatures. Cut the meat into portions or roast it.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:shulker_meat_slice" /> Cut on the cutting board with a knife using 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:shulker_meat" />. Yield 2.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:stir_fried_shulker_meat" /> Combine in a heated cooking pot using 1 <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:onion" /> or another accepted onion plus 1 <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:tomato" /> or another accepted tomato plus 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:chorus_fruit" /> or an accepted substitute plus 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:shulker_meat" /> or another accepted shulker meat. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:raw_ender_mite_meat" /> Obtain from endermite while holding a Farmer’s Delight knife. Keep this as an input for <ItemLink id="ends_delight:dried_endermite_meat" />. Its recipe uses cook on a campfire.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:dried_endermite_meat" /> Cook in a furnace using 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:raw_ender_mite_meat" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:roasted_shulker_meat" /> Cook in a furnace using 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:shulker_meat" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:roasted_shulker_meat_slice" /> Cut on the cutting board with a knife using 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:roasted_shulker_meat" />. Yield 2.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:grilled_shulker" /> This entry is a serving, container or specialized ingredient rather than a complete meal recipe. Inspect its serving action or tooltip before planning production.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:enderman_gristle" /> Obtain from enderman while holding a Farmer’s Delight knife. Keep this as an input for <ItemLink id="ends_delight:enderman_gristle_stew" />. Its recipe uses combine in a heated cooking pot.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:raw_ender_sausage" /> Combine in the crafting grid using 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:raw_dragon_meat_cuts" /> or another accepted raw dragon meat plus 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:enderman_gristle" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:chorus_sauce" />. Yield 2.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:ender_sausage" /> Cook in a furnace using 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:raw_ender_sausage" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:enderman_gristle_stew" /> Combine in a heated cooking pot using 2 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:enderman_gristle" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:dried_chorus_flower" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:chorus_succulent" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:shulker_soup" /> Combine in a heated cooking pot using 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:shulker_meat" /> or another accepted shulker meat plus 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:dried_chorus_flower" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:chorus_succulent" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:shulker_omelette_mixture" /> Combine in the crafting grid using 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:egg" /> or another accepted eggs plus 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:shulker_meat" /> or another accepted shulker meat plus 1 <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:onion" /> or another accepted onion. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:shulker_omelette" /> Cook in a furnace using 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:shulker_omelette_mixture" />. Yield 1.

- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:dragon_leg" /> Obtain from ender dragon. Keep this as an input for <ItemLink id="ends_delight:raw_dragon_meat" />. Its recipe uses cut on the cutting board with a knife.

- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:liquid_dragon_egg" /> Cut on the cutting board with a knife using 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:non_hatchable_dragon_egg" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:half_dragon_egg_shell" /> Cut on the cutting board with a knife using 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:non_hatchable_dragon_egg" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:non_hatchable_dragon_egg" /> Arrange in the crafting grid using 4 <ItemLink id="minecraft:obsidian" /> plus 4 <ItemLink id="minecraft:end_crystal" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:dragon_tooth" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:roasted_dragon_meat" /> Cut on the cutting board with a knife using 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:smoked_dragon_leg" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:raw_dragon_meat" /> The Ender Dragon supplies this ingredient. This branch requires a dragon encounter, unlike gathering chorus fruit on the outer islands.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:roasted_dragon_meat_cuts" /> Cut on the cutting board with a knife using 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:roasted_dragon_meat" />. Yield 3.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:raw_dragon_meat_cuts" /> Cut on the cutting board with a knife using 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:raw_dragon_meat" />. Yield 3.

Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:ender_congee" /> Combine in a heated cooking pot using 1 <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:rice" /> or another accepted rice plus 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:dried_endermite_meat" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="ends_delight:ender_pearl_grain" />. Yield 1. Serve in <ItemLink id="minecraft:bowl" />.
- <ItemLink id="ends_delight:dragon_leg_with_sauce" /> This entry is a serving, container or specialized ingredient rather than a complete meal recipe. Inspect its serving action or tooltip before planning production.
