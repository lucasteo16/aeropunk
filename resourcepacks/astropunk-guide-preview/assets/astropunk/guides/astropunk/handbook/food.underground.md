# Underground food

## Cave carrots

<EmiSearch query="@minersdelight" />

![A kitchen in the caves](images/visual-cavern-kitchen.png)

A kitchen in the caves

<ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:wild_cave_carrots" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:cave_carrot" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:baked_cave_carrot" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:cave_soup" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:cave_hamburger" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:copper_carrot" /></ItemGrid>

Miner's Delight builds its menu on a cave crop. <ItemLink id="minersdelight:wild_cave_carrots" /> grows in cave patches rather than on a surface farm. Break a patch to start your own row, then plant the harvested <ItemLink id="minersdelight:cave_carrot" /> underground.

Bake the carrot for a simple provision, or use it in a soup and a burger. <ItemLink id="minersdelight:copper_carrot" /> is a separate prepared item, not a cooked cave carrot.

| Stage | Item | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wild patch | <ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:wild_cave_carrots" /></ItemGrid> | Seed source |
| Harvest | <ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:cave_carrot" /></ItemGrid> | Planting and cooking |
| Baked | <ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:baked_cave_carrot" /></ItemGrid> | Portable food |
| Combined | <ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:cave_soup" /></ItemGrid> | Full meal in a bowl |

## Underground proteins

<ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:arthropod" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:spider_leg" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:bat_wing" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:smoked_bat_wing" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:silverfish_eggs" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:bat_rolls" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:insect_stew" /></ItemGrid>

Spiders and cave arthropods supply <ItemLink id="minersdelight:arthropod" /> and <ItemLink id="minersdelight:spider_leg" />. Bats supply <ItemLink id="minersdelight:bat_wing" />, which smokes into a longer lasting provision. <ItemLink id="minersdelight:silverfish_eggs" /> come from infested blocks rather than from a farm.

Handle these as a separate supply chain from crop food. A mining trip that clears a spider nest and a bat cluster returns with meat, and the eggs arrive only when you break infested stone.

| Ingredient | Source | Prepared form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arthropod | Spiders and cave insects | Cooked arthropod and insect stew |
| Spider leg | Spiders | Baked spider leg |
| Bat wing | Bats | Smoked bat wing |
| Silverfish eggs | Infested blocks | Weird caviar and seasoned arthropods |

## Copper kitchen

<ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:copper_pot" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:copper_cup" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:beef_stew_cup" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:milk_cup" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:water_cup" /></ItemGrid>

The <ItemLink id="minersdelight:copper_pot" /> is the station for this menu. <ItemLink id="minersdelight:copper_cup" /> is a serving container, and a cup dish has its own recipe. Fill cups before you go down, because <ItemLink id="minersdelight:water_cup" /> and <ItemLink id="minersdelight:milk_cup" /> are container contents, not cooked meals.

Cups free a hand compared with a bowl and they stack the way a bowl meal does not. Choose the serving form before the trip rather than after the pot is full.

***

## Provision bars

<ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:crunchy_bar" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:nutritional_bar" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:golden_nutritional_bar" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:gossypium" /><ItemIcon id="minecraft:honey_bottle" /></ItemGrid>

Crafted bars are the travel branch. <ItemLink id="minersdelight:crunchy_bar" /> is the basic bar, <ItemLink id="minersdelight:nutritional_bar" /> is the denser version, and <ItemLink id="minersdelight:golden_nutritional_bar" /> is the gold tier. They do not need a bowl and they keep their stack in a packed inventory.

<ItemLink id="minersdelight:gossypium" /> supplies the plant fibre for the bar recipes. Grow it with the cave carrot row so a mining base can restock without returning to the surface.

| Provision | Container | Best use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Baked cave carrot | None | Short trip snack |
| Cave soup | Bowl or cup | Full meal at a camp |
| Crunchy bar | None | Deep mining ration |
| Golden nutritional bar | None | Long expedition reserve |

## Squid and plant meals

<ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:squid" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:baked_squid" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:tentacles" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:takoyaki" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:vegan_patty" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:vegan_hamburger" /><ItemIcon id="minersdelight:plate_of_fake_meatloaf" /></ItemGrid>

Glow squid and squid supply <ItemLink id="minersdelight:squid" /> and <ItemLink id="minersdelight:tentacles" />, which become baked squid and takoyaki. The plant branch uses <ItemLink id="minersdelight:vegan_patty" /> for a meat free burger and meatloaf. That branch does not mean every recipe accepts a vegetable substitute.
