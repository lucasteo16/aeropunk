Wild Cave Carrots provide the underground crop branch. Baking makes a prepared carrot, while soup and burger recipes combine the crop with other ingredients. Bars are separate crafted provisions.

- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:wild_cave_carrots" /> Keep this as an input for <ItemLink id="minersdelight:gossypium" />. Its recipe uses cut on the cutting board with a knife.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:baked_cave_carrot" /> Cook in a furnace using 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:cave_carrot" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:cave_carrot" /> Cut on the cutting board with a knife using 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:wild_cave_carrots" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:cave_hamburger" /> Combine in the crafting grid using 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:bread" /> or another accepted bread plus 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:crunchy_bar" /> or an accepted substitute plus 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:smoked_bat_wing" /> plus 2 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:baked_tentacles" /> or an accepted substitute. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:cave_soup" /> Combine in a heated cooking pot using 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:cave_carrot" /> or another accepted cave carrot plus 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:red_mushroom" /> plus 2 <ItemLink id="minecraft:brown_mushroom" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:cave_soup_cup" /> This entry is a serving, container or specialized ingredient rather than a complete meal recipe. Inspect its serving action or tooltip before planning production.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:copper_carrot" /> Arrange in the crafting grid using 8 <ItemLink id="minecraft:copper_ingot" /> or another accepted copper plus 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:cave_carrot" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:crunchy_bar" /> Combine in the crafting grid using 3 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:cooked_arthropod" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:cooked_arthropod" /> or an accepted substitute plus 3 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:silverfish_eggs" />. Yield 4.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:golden_nutritional_bar" /> Arrange in the crafting grid using 8 <ItemLink id="minecraft:gold_nugget" /> or another accepted gold plus 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:nutritional_bar" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:nutritional_bar" /> Combine in the crafting grid using 4 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:crunchy_bar" /> plus 2 <ItemLink id="minecraft:wheat" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:honey_bottle" />. Yield 4.

Knife hunting supplies arthropods and bat wings. Infested-block mining supplies Silverfish Eggs through a chance-based drop. These sources differ from ordinary farm eggs and crops.

- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:arthropod" /> Use a knife on a bee, silverfish or endermite that is not burning. Cook the harvested ingredient before choosing insect dishes.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:baked_spider_leg" /> Cook in a furnace using 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:spider_leg" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:bat_cookie" /> Combine in the crafting grid using 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:smoked_bat_wing" /> plus 2 <ItemLink id="minecraft:wheat" /> or another accepted wheat. Yield 8.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:bat_rolls" /> Combine in a heated cooking pot using 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:bat_wing" /> or another accepted bat wing plus 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:chicken" /> or another accepted raw meat or an accepted substitute. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:bat_soup" /> Combine in a heated cooking pot using 2 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:bat_wing" /> or another accepted bat wing. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:bat_soup_cup" /> This entry is a serving, container or specialized ingredient rather than a complete meal recipe. Inspect its serving action or tooltip before planning production.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:bat_wing" /> Knife hunting a bat that is not burning yields two wings. Smoking and combined recipes turn them into prepared food.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:cooked_arthropod" /> Cut on the cutting board with a knife using 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:baked_spider_leg" />. Yield 2.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:insect_sandwich" /> Combine in the crafting grid using 2 <ItemLink id="minecraft:bread" /> or another accepted bread plus 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:baked_spider_leg" /> or another accepted cooked insect meat plus 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:baked_spider_leg" /> or another accepted cooked insect meat or an accepted substitute. Yield 2.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:insect_stew" /> Combine in a heated cooking pot using 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:crunchy_bar" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:arthropod" /> or an accepted substitute. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:insect_stew_cup" /> This entry is a serving, container or specialized ingredient rather than a complete meal recipe. Inspect its serving action or tooltip before planning production.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:insect_wrap" /> Combine in the crafting grid using 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:bread" /> or another accepted bread plus 1 <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:onion" /> or another accepted onion or an accepted substitute plus 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:crunchy_bar" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:plate_of_glazed_arachnid_limbs" /> This entry is a serving, container or specialized ingredient rather than a complete meal recipe. Inspect its serving action or tooltip before planning production.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:seasoned_arthropods" /> Combine in the crafting grid using 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:bowl" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:cooked_arthropod" /> or an accepted substitute plus 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:weird_caviar" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:silverfish_eggs" /> Mining an accepted infested block with a pickaxe has a 35 percent added-drop chance. Check the food recipe before treating this as an ordinary egg substitute.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:smoked_bat_wing" /> Cook in a furnace using 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:bat_wing" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:spider_leg" /> Obtain from spider while holding a Farmer’s Delight knife. Keep this as an input for <ItemLink id="minersdelight:baked_spider_leg" />. Its recipe uses cook in a furnace.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:weird_caviar" /> Combine in the crafting grid using 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:bowl" /> plus 3 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:silverfish_eggs" />. Yield 1.

Squid ingredients come from aquatic hunting, even though this menu belongs to Miner’s Delight. Cutting and cooking prepare squid portions. Plant-based patties supply a separate meal branch, not a claim that every meat recipe accepts vegetables.

- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:baked_squid" /> Cook in a furnace using 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:squid" /> or an accepted substitute. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:baked_tentacles" /> Cut on the cutting board with a knife using 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:baked_squid" />. Yield 3.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:bowl_of_stuffed_squid" /> This entry is a serving, container or specialized ingredient rather than a complete meal recipe. Inspect its serving action or tooltip before planning production.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:glow_squid" /> Obtain from glow squid while holding a Farmer’s Delight knife. Keep this as an input for <ItemLink id="minersdelight:baked_squid" />. Its recipe uses cook in a furnace.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:pasta_with_veggieballs" /> Combine in a heated cooking pot using 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:baked_cave_carrot" /> or another accepted baked cave carrot plus 1 <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:raw_pasta" /> or another accepted pasta plus 1 <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:tomato_sauce" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:plate_of_fake_meatloaf" /> This entry is a serving, container or specialized ingredient rather than a complete meal recipe. Inspect its serving action or tooltip before planning production.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:squid" /> Knife hunting a squid that is not burning supplies the whole squid ingredient. Cut or cook it before assembling squid meals.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:squid_sandwich" /> Combine in the crafting grid using 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:baked_squid" /> or another accepted cooked squid plus 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:bread" /> or another accepted bread. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:takoyaki" /> Combine in a heated cooking pot using 2 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:tentacles" /> or another accepted tentacles plus 1 <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:wheat_dough" /> or another accepted dough plus 1 <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:onion" /> or another accepted onion. Yield 1. Serve in <ItemLink id="minecraft:bowl" />.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:tentacles" /> Cut on the cutting board with a knife using 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:glow_squid" />. Yield 3.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:tentacles_on_a_stick" /> Combine in the crafting grid using 1 <ItemLink id="spawn:suspicious_stick" /> or another accepted wooden plus 2 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:baked_tentacles" />. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:vegan_hamburger" /> Combine in the crafting grid using 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:bread" /> or another accepted bread plus 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:vegan_patty" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:cabbage" /> or another accepted cabbage plus 1 <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:tomato" /> or another accepted tomato plus 1 <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:onion" /> or another accepted onion. Yield 1.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:vegan_patty" /> Cut on the cutting board with a knife using 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:baked_cave_carrot" />. Yield 2.
- <ItemLink id="minersdelight:vegan_steak_and_potatoes" /> Combine in the crafting grid using 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:baked_potato" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="minersdelight:baked_cave_carrot" /> or another accepted baked cave carrot plus 1 <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:onion" /> or another accepted onion plus 1 <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:cooked_rice" /> plus 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:bowl" />.
