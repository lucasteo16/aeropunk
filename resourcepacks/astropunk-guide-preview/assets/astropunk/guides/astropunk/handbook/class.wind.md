# Wind Wizard

![YouTube](images/tutorial-video.png) [English](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Minecraft+1.21.1+Elemental+Wizards+RPG+Wind+Wizard+abilities+equipment+tutorial+English) · [中文](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=%E6%88%91%E7%9A%84%E4%B8%96%E7%95%8C+1.21.1+Elemental+Wizards+RPG+%E9%A3%8E%E7%B3%BB%E6%B3%95%E5%B8%88+%E6%8A%80%E8%83%BD+%E8%A3%85%E5%A4%87+%E6%95%99%E7%A8%8B+%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87)

## Role & first weapon

Wind Wizards use air wands and staves for aimed attacks, persistent areas and self effects.

![Elemental Wizard robes](images/visual-elemental-robes.png)

### What to look for

Air spells scale with Air power. The Feather Wand names Air power and costs a feather and a stick, so the first focus is available from the first chickens you meet. Read the school line before you bind a book.

The Air Wand and the Air Staff are the specialist upgrades. The Air Wand uses a phantom membrane, which means a night under open sky or a trade, and the staff continues that line. The Elemental Staff names Air, Earth and Water power at once but needs a nether star, so it is a late goal.

The Wind Robe supports the school with wind charges and wool, and its chest piece also needs gold. The Hurricane Robe is a later upgrade that uses an epic armor upgrade and a warden upgrade crystal. The Elemental Robe covers all three elemental schools.

Air abilities move and hold space. Twister and Tornado are areas that stay on the field, while Updraft and Windfield need a target. A build that pushes enemies away and then drops an area on the ground needs runes for both casts.

A successful first use looks like this. Hold the Feather Wand, cast at a hostile that is chasing you and see it lose ground or stop, then cast Twister where it stands. If the target does not move, check that the spell is the aimed one rather than the ground one.

### Readiness

The stages below are optional. They describe access and capability, not a required order.

| Stage | Ready when | Next decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early game | You have a feather, a stick and the rune the wand spell names. | Craft the Feather Wand and cast once at a hostile. |
| Early midgame | A Spell Binding Table with bookshelves is reachable and its cost is payable. | Bind the Wind book and equip it with a compatible focus held. |
| Midgame | Wind charges and a phantom membrane are available to you. | Compare the Wind Robe with the Air Staff, and keep the general robe if its bonus serves your casts better. |
| Late game | You can obtain an epic armor upgrade or a nether star. | Choose one goal at a time and check the school bonus on the result before replacing a working piece. |
