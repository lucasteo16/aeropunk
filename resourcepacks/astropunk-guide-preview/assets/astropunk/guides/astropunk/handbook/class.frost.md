![YouTube](images/tutorial-video.png) [English](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Minecraft+1.21.1+Wizards+RPG+Frost+Wizard+abilities+equipment+tutorial+English) · [中文](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=%E6%88%91%E7%9A%84%E4%B8%96%E7%95%8C+1.21.1+Wizards+RPG+%E5%86%B0%E9%9C%9C%E6%B3%95%E5%B8%88+%E6%8A%80%E8%83%BD+%E8%A3%85%E5%A4%87+%E6%95%99%E7%A8%8B+%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87)

## Role & first weapon

Frost Wizards use frost wands and staves for area attacks, protection and projectiles.

![Frost spell effects](images/visual-frost-magic.png)

### What to look for

Frost spells scale with Frost power. The Frost Wand names Frost power, and the general Wizard Staff names Arcane, Fire and Frost power together, so a wand keeps the school focused. Read the school line before you bind a book.

The Frost Wand recipe uses an iron ingot and a snowball, which makes it one of the cheapest school focuses in the mod. The Frost Staff adds prismarine crystals, so it waits for an ocean visit. The staff also carries a stronger weapon spell than the wand, and the tooltip shows what it costs.

The general Wizard Robe covers any school with wool and lapis lazuli. The Frost Robe uses a prismarine shard per piece, so it follows the same ocean trip as the staff. A Netherite Frost Robe piece is a smithing upgrade of the matching Frost Robe piece.

Frost Shield is a self effect in the book, so a frost build can hold a position while its area spells land. Pair the focus with the rune type its spells name, in the inventory or in an equipped pouch, and keep enough for a shield cast as well as an attack.

A successful first use looks like this. Hold the Frost Wand, gather two or three hostiles in front of you, cast the wand spell and see the rune count drop while every target in the area reacts. If only one target reacts, the spell is aimed rather than an area spell, which the tooltip states.

### Readiness

The order is optional. Each row describes a condition, not a required chapter.

| Stage | Ready when | Next decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early game | You have an iron ingot, a snowball and the rune the wand spell names. | Craft the Frost Wand and cast once at a hostile. |
| Early midgame | You can reach a Spell Binding Table with bookshelves and pay the displayed cost. | Bind the Frost book and equip it with a compatible focus held. |
| Midgame | Prismarine shards and crystals are available from an ocean trip. | Compare the Frost Robe with the Frost Staff. Keep the general robe if the school bonus on the specialist piece does not help your spells. |
| Late game | You hold a netherite upgrade smithing template and netherite ingots. | Upgrade one Frost Robe piece at a time, then compare the result with the piece you replace. |
