## Class book

<EmiSearch query="@create" /> <EmiSearch query="@runes" /> <EmiSearch query="@spell_engine" />

<ItemLink id="spell_engine:spell_book" />

Create the Warrior book from a normal book at the Spell Binding Table. Each is a configured Spell Book, not a separate item type. Meet the displayed experience-level cost, then bind the listed abilities with lapis lazuli and the required bookshelf power. Equip the book in its supported slot and hold a compatible weapon or focus.

- [Spell binding & resources](combat.magic.md) Costs, weapon compatibility, runes and casting controls.
