### Readiness & first fight

Warriors use heavy, slow weapons, and the publisher lists double axes and glaives for the Warrior Codex. Each swing commits you, so the class pays off when you choose the moment and use charge or shouts to close the distance. Warrior armor is the entry set and Berserker armor is the heavier family in the same list. A good first fight is a charge into two mobs followed by one area ability, ending with you still standing and the group thinned.

The stages below are optional. They describe what you need on hand and what each step unlocks, not how long you have played.

| Stage | Ready when | Suggested action or next decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early game: first heavy weapon | A double axe or a glaive, food, and room to swing. | Craft the iron double axe. Practise the swing timing on a single mob before fighting groups. |
| Early midgame: warrior codex | A Spell Binding Table, the Warrior Codex, and its learning cost. | Bind the codex. Keep a thrown or charging ability ready for the moment a mob backs away. |
| Midgame: armor | Warrior armor pieces, or berserker armor pieces. | Complete the warrior set first. Treat berserker armor as a separate choice, not a free upgrade. |
| Late game: netherite | Netherite materials and a berserker armor piece. | Upgrade the berserker set only if you intend to stay in melee. Otherwise spend the materials on your weapon path. |
