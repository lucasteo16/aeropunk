# Seafood

## Starcatcher's Delight

<EmiSearch query="@farmersdelight" /> <EmiSearch query="@starcatcher" />

<EmiSearch query="@starcatcher_delight" />

![Starcatcher dishes and rarity-tier meals](images/visual-starcatcher-cuisine.png)

Starcatcher dishes and rarity-tier meals

<ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="starcatcher_delight:starcaught_fillet_common" /><ItemIcon id="starcatcher_delight:hosomaki_common" /><ItemIcon id="starcatcher_delight:fish_and_chips_common" /><ItemIcon id="starcatcher_delight:cactifish_stew" /><ItemIcon id="starcatcher_delight:blossom_toast" /><ItemIcon id="starcatcher_delight:honey_taiyaki" /></ItemGrid>

This addon turns Starcatcher catches into Farmer's Delight food. A fillet carries the rarity of the fish it came from, so a common fillet and a legendary fillet are different ingredients rather than one item with a quality tag.

| Family | Members and what decides the result |
| --- | --- |
| Starcaught fillets | Common, uncommon, rare, epic and legendary fillets. Each tier is its own item and its own ingredient. |
| Sushi and meals | Hosomaki, Nigiri, Temaki, Uramaki, Fish Salad, Healthy Fish Omelette and Fish and Chips all have one dish per rarity tier. |
| Habitat dishes | Cactifish Stew, Sludge Stew, Blossom Toast, Steamed Redscaled Tuna, Grilled Shroomfish, Spore Nigiri, Weather Soup and Magma Fish Balls name a specific catch. |
| Taiyaki | Sweet berry, glow berry, chocolate, honey, pumpkin, watermelon and suspicious variants share one cake shape. |

<Recipe id="starcatcher_delight:hosomaki_common" />

One hosomaki batch takes one common fillet, one kelp and two rice and returns four rolls. The tier in the name is not decoration. A legendary fillet cannot stand in for a common one, so fillet the fish whose rarity the recipe names.

## Hybrid Delights

<EmiSearch query="@hybrid_aquatic" />

<EmiSearch query="@hybrid_delights" />

![Hybrid Delights ingredients and meals](images/visual-hybrid-cuisine.png)

Hybrid Delights ingredients and meals

<ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="hybrid_aquatic:raw_fish_meat" /><ItemIcon id="hybrid_delights:ray_wing" /><ItemIcon id="hybrid_delights:carp_roe" /><ItemIcon id="hybrid_delights:shrimp_fried_rice" /><ItemIcon id="hybrid_delights:lobster_stew" /><ItemIcon id="hybrid_delights:caviar_toast" /></ItemGrid>

| Family | Members and preparation |
| --- | --- |
| Fish portions | Raw Fish Meat, fish steaks and Ray Wings. Cutting Tuna gives steaks, cutting Stingray gives Ray Wings. |
| Roe and preservation | Roe, cured roe, Caviar Toast, salted fish, pickled Herring and pickled Bull Kelp. |
| Fish meals | Fish Kebab, Fish N Chips, Fisherman's Pie, Fisherman's Soup, Eel Noodles, Masgouf and tuna dishes. |
| Shellfish | Shrimp Fried Rice, Lobster Stew, Baked Stuffed Lobster, Crab Cake, Crab Rangoon, Bisque and Chowder. |
| Gelatin foods | Fish Gelatin leads to Jelly, Jelly Toast, Jellied Carp and flavoured Fishsicles. |

Two pots are involved and they are not interchangeable. Lobster Stew is registered as a Farm and Charm pot cooking recipe, so it needs that Let's Do pot rather than the Farmer's Delight cooking pot. The other pot dishes use the Farmer's Delight cooking pot. Cutting Cod, Salmon or Carp can also drop roe as a chance output, so treat roe as a bonus rather than a guaranteed return.

## Ocean's Delight

<EmiSearch query="@oceansdelight" />

![Ocean ingredients and prepared dishes](images/visual-ocean-cuisine.png)

Ocean ingredients and prepared dishes

<ItemGrid><ItemIcon id="oceansdelight:tentacles" /><ItemIcon id="oceansdelight:guardian_tail" /><ItemIcon id="oceansdelight:elder_guardian_slab" /><ItemIcon id="oceansdelight:cooked_stuffed_squid" /><ItemIcon id="oceansdelight:elder_guardian_roll" /><ItemIcon id="oceansdelight:seagrass_salad" /></ItemGrid>

| Ingredient family | Meals and sources |
| --- | --- |
| Squid | Tentacles and Cut Tentacles support Squid Rings, Stuffed Squid and Tentacle on a Stick. |
| Guardians | Guardian Tail and Elder Guardian portions support Guardian Soup, Elder Guardian Roll and Cabbage Wrapped Elder Guardian. |
| Cod and pufferfish | Stuffed Cod, Kelp Encrusted Cod, Fugu Slice and Fugu Roll use their own preparation. |
| Marine plants | Seagrass Salad, Braised Sea Pickle and Honey Fried Kelp use plant ingredients. |

Guardian ingredients come from monument encounters, not from a rod. Decide whether the meal is worth a monument run before you plan it, and keep prepared pufferfish separate from raw pufferfish.

## Cooking and carrying

The cutting board, stove, pot and skillet work the same way here as in the rest of the pack. Seafood adds ingredients, not a second kitchen. Short Stacks applies to edible fish and finished dishes alike, so check food value and stack limit before packing a long trip.
