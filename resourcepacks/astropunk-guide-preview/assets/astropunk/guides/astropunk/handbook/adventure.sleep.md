# Sleep

## Rest while travelling

Sleeping bags provide portable night-time rest without changing your home respawn point. The pack enables their recipes. This white example uses three white wool blocks, other colors use their matching wool.

## Hammocks & time

Hammocks provide daytime rest that turns day into night, without changing your respawn point. The pack allows hammocks during the day and sleeping bags at night, server settings can change those rules.

Better Days controls day and night duration and can accelerate time while players sleep. The server determines its active time settings, installing the mod does not imply a particular day length or that every processing machine accelerates with sleep.
